# Regional Considerations

## Australia & New Zealand
- Strong preference for governed, auditable systems
- Smartsheet and Microsoft ecosystems are common
- Clear data ownership and privacy expectations

## APAC
- Multi-market and multi-entity complexity is the norm
- Need for standardisation with local flexibility
- High-volume CX use cases (especially quick commerce and telecom)

## Middle East
- Rapid digital transformation in retail and hospitality
- Omnichannel and loyalty are high-priority investment areas
- Increasing focus on data quality and customer journey orchestration
