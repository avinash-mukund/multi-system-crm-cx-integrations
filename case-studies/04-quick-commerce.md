# Case Study 04 – Quick Commerce

**Industry:** Quick Commerce  
**Systems:** Kapture CX + supporting operational systems  
**Focus:** High-volume contact centre performance and customer experience

## Challenge
High-volume contact centre operations on a legacy stack were causing response time SLAs to slip and CSAT to fluctuate during peak demand windows.

## Approach
- Mapped peak demand patterns and failure points
- Redesigned workflows for faster first-contact resolution
- Introduced better categorisation and routing
- Supported migration and stabilisation activities

## Outcomes
- Meaningful reduction in average response time
- Improvement in CSAT and first-contact resolution
- More stable performance during peak windows

## Key Lessons
- In high-volume environments, workflow design and categorisation discipline matter more than features
- Peak-load behaviour must be designed for explicitly
