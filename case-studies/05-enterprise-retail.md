# Case Study 05 – Enterprise Retail

**Industry:** Enterprise Retail & E-commerce  
**Systems:** Multiple (CRM, ERP, CDP, POS, CX)  
**Focus:** Omnichannel consistency and cross-channel attribution

## Challenge
Online and offline touchpoints were operating in silos. Inventory, customer data, and contact centre operations lacked consistent views, making personalisation and attribution difficult.

## Approach
- Focused on creating a more coherent customer view across channels
- Improved data flows between ERP, CRM, CDP and CX systems
- Strengthened post-purchase and loyalty recognition
- Supported measurement of cross-channel impact

## Outcomes
- Better cross-channel attribution
- Improved repeat purchase and customer lifetime value signals
- Clearer operational ownership across channels

## Key Lessons
- Omnichannel is primarily a data and process problem, not just a channel problem
- Attribution only becomes useful when underlying customer identity is reliable
