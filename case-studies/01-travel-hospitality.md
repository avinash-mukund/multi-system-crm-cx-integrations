# Case Study 01 – Travel & Hospitality

**Industry:** Travel & Hospitality (Mid-market)  
**Systems:** HubSpot ↔ Kapture CX  
**Focus:** Pre-cruise booking flows and onboard customer experience workflows

## Challenge
Disconnected booking and onboard service processes leading to poor handoff between shore-side teams and onboard staff. Limited visibility of customer context at the point of service.

## Approach
- Modelled the full customer journey from booking to post-cruise
- Built object relationships and lifecycle stages in HubSpot
- Created automated handoff workflows into Kapture CX
- Established data quality rules for key customer and booking fields

## Outcomes
- Improved continuity of customer context from booking to onboard service
- Reduced manual handoffs and missed information
- Clearer ownership across shore and ship teams

## Key Lessons
- Journey-stage alignment between CRM and CX systems is critical
- Data quality at the point of booking has downstream impact on service quality
