# Case Study 02 – Telecom (Regional)

**Industry:** Telecommunications  
**Systems:** Salesforce ↔ Kapture CX  
**Focus:** Multi-market contact centre rollout and integration

## Challenge
Regional contact centres operating with inconsistent processes and fragmented customer data across markets.

## Approach
- Standardised core ticket and customer objects
- Designed Salesforce ↔ Kapture CX bridge
- Implemented shared workflows while allowing local market flexibility
- Focused on data governance during rollout

## Outcomes
- More consistent customer handling across markets
- Improved visibility for regional management
- Cleaner foundation for future automation

## Key Lessons
- Balance standardisation with local market needs
- Data governance must be designed in from the start of multi-market rollouts
