# Case Study 03 – Premium Retail

**Industry:** Premium Retail (Mid-market)  
**Systems:** Microsoft Dynamics (MSD) ERP ↔ HubSpot ↔ Kapture CX  
**Focus:** In-store loyalty and post-purchase engagement

## Challenge
Loyalty data and customer context were inconsistent between online and offline channels. Point-of-sale and post-purchase engagement systems were not reliably connected, creating gaps in personalisation and service.

## Approach
- Integrated MSD ERP with HubSpot as the engagement layer
- Connected Kapture CX for service and post-purchase workflows
- Focused on data quality rules at the point of sale and order creation
- Designed loyalty recognition that worked across online and in-store journeys

## Outcomes
- More accurate loyalty recognition at point of sale
- Improved post-purchase engagement consistency
- Reduced manual reconciliation between systems

## Key Lessons
- Offline + online data quality must be solved together
- Loyalty value is only realised when recognition is reliable at the moment of truth (POS)
