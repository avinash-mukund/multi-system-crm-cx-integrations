# Blueprint: Loyalty & POS Sync

**Use case:** Retail organisations needing reliable loyalty recognition at point of sale and consistent post-purchase engagement.

## Pattern
- ERP / POS as transactional system of record
- CRM as engagement and loyalty profile layer
- CX platform for service and post-purchase workflows

## Critical Success Factors
- Real-time or near real-time loyalty balance/visibility at POS
- Strong customer matching rules
- Clear fallback when identity is uncertain
