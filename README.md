# Multi-System CRM & CX Integration Playbook

Practical patterns for connecting CRMs, ERPs, ticketing, and loyalty systems — drawn from real mid-market and enterprise implementations across Travel, Retail, Telecom, and Quick Commerce.

**Relevant markets:** Australia / New Zealand • APAC • Middle East

---

## Who This Is For
- Product Managers, Technical Product Managers, and Solutions Consultants
- Teams integrating HubSpot, Salesforce, Microsoft Dynamics, or regional CRMs with ticketing and ERP systems
- Organisations dealing with data quality, lifecycle automation, and omnichannel consistency

---

## What’s Inside

### Implementation Case Studies
1. **Travel & Hospitality** – Pre-cruise booking to onboard CX (HubSpot ↔ Kapture CX)
2. **Telecom** – Multi-market contact centre and Salesforce ↔ CX bridge
3. **Premium Retail** – MSD ERP + HubSpot + loyalty at point of sale
4. **Quick Commerce** – High-volume CX transformation
5. **Enterprise Retail** – Omnichannel consistency and attribution

### Core Themes
- Object modelling and lifecycle automation
- Data quality and governance across systems
- Configurable vs custom integration decisions
- Common failure points and how they were resolved
- Handover and client enablement
